Download for Android

Free · Android 7.0+ · other builds

Installing

  1. Tap Download for Android above — you'll get an .apk file.
  2. Open it. Android asks permission to install unknown apps; allow it for your browser, then tap Install.
    Expected — it just means the app doesn't come from the Play Store.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, choose Install anyway.

What it does

TodayOnly what's due right now — mark each done or not done.
RemindersNamed by subject and topic, repeating daily until actioned.
AttachmentsImages, video, documents, web and YouTube links per topic.
CalendarCompleted revisions behind you, scheduled ones ahead.
ProgressStreaks, activity and per-subject mastery.
Private by designNo accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no ads.
